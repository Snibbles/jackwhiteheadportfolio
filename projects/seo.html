SEO · Automation

SEO link-building tool

Link building is slow when you do it by hand. I built a tool in Google Sheets and Google Apps Script that finds sites linking to a client's competitors, scores them, drafts the outreach and tracks every price and status in one dashboard.

Role
Design & build
For
Client campaigns at Klein Media
Tools
Google Sheets, Google Apps Script

how it works

From competitor links to a priced shortlist.

  1. Pull the sites that link to the client's competitors.
  2. Score each one on topical relevance, domain authority and traffic.
  3. Draft the outreach e-mails and track price and status per site.
  4. Follow the whole campaign on one dashboard.

what it saves

Hours, not subscriptions.

In one live campaign the tool scored 179 prospects and had 178 outreach e-mails ready; 128 were sent and 44 prices confirmed. By my estimate a campaign takes 30 to 60 minutes instead of about 8 hours, and a full analysis costs a couple of dollars in API credit, where a traditional SEO subscription runs around €100 a month.

Alongside the tool I do on-site SEO for clients: keyword research, sitemap and page structure, and technical audits.
